## Outreach angles

### Editorial resource pitch

Subject: A practical way to compare custom packaging quotes on equal terms

Packaging buyers often receive quotes based on different dimensions, carton assumptions, one-time charges and delivery boundaries. We created a free browser-based RFQ builder plus a downloadable 27-field worksheet that keeps the specification fixed and requires suppliers to state deviations. It cites UPS and FedEx guidance for dimensional weight, ICC Incoterms 2020 and the ISTA 3A overview. If useful to your readers, we can provide an original, non-promotional explanation of the comparison method. No exclusive claims or fabricated benchmark data will be used.

### Directory application note

Use the exact company name, address, contact details and documented product categories. Supply registration, facility or process evidence only when current and verifiable. Avoid claims such as “certified,” “food grade,” “recyclable” or “tested” unless the specific scope and issuing evidence can be disclosed.

## 90-day cadence

- Weeks 1–2: verify legal identity and evidence pack; publish and test the three linkable assets; prepare one short editor pitch and one directory profile.
- Weeks 3–6: contact no more than five highly relevant organizations per week; personalize every pitch; record the public page, contact method, response and outcome.
- Weeks 7–10: create one additional evidence-led asset based on genuine buyer questions or anonymized first-party operational data, only if the data can be documented.
- Weeks 11–13: reclaim unlinked brand mentions, repair legitimate broken links pointing to the domain and evaluate referral sessions and qualified RFQs rather than raw backlink count.

## Measurement

Track the referring domain, target URL, discovery date, editorial context, follow/nofollow status, referral sessions, RFQ starts and qualified inquiries. Review link quality by relevance and real referral value; do not use Domain Rating alone as a success metric.
