Buyer FAQ

RFQ builder questions

What information should a custom packaging RFQ include?

Include the product and packed dimensions, packaging format, material or performance requirement, quantity, print, finishes, insert or closure, artwork status, destination, trade term and approval requirements.

Does this builder store buyer data?

No. It runs locally in the browser. The brief is transmitted only after the buyer deliberately opens WhatsApp or an email client.

Is dimensional weight the same for every carrier?

No. Divisors, measurement rules and rounding can vary. Confirm current rules for the selected carrier, service, origin and destination.

Can this tool provide a final freight price?

No. It estimates dimensional weight only. Final freight also depends on actual packed weight, carton count, route, current tariffs and accessorial charges.
