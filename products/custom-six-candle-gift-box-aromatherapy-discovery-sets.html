How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Jar capacity is not enough to size the tray. Provide the actual jar body, lid overhang, filled weight and clearance requirement. Final board, insert and closure choices depend on the approved sample and distribution method.

Buyer FAQ

Does the quotation include the candles?

No. This page is for the custom gift box and insert. Candle jars are shown only to explain the six-cavity packaging application.

Can the insert be changed from six jars to another layout?

Yes. Cavity count and spacing can be redesigned for the supplied jar dimensions, product weight and presentation order.

How do I prevent candle jars from touching?

The dieline should include individual cavity walls, controlled clearances and a top-clearance check before sample approval.

What information is needed for a quote?

Send jar dimensions, lid dimensions, filled weight, quantity, preferred box style, artwork, finish references and destination country.
