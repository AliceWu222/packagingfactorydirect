How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Fine floral lines and small type may fill in or crack on unsuitable paper. Supply vector artwork and approve an emboss proof or physical sample before bulk production.

Buyer FAQ

Are these clothing items or packaging tags?

They are custom paper hang tags for branding and product presentation. Clothing, jewelry and other retail goods are not included.

What artwork format is best for embossing?

Vector artwork with separated emboss, print and foil layers is preferred so line weight and registration can be reviewed.

Can the tag include a string or cord?

Yes. Specify attachment material, color, length, knot or fastener and the required hole size.

What should be sent for a quote?

Send tag dimensions, paper preference, quantity, vector artwork, emboss area, print or foil colors, hole position, string details and destination.
