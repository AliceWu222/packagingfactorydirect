How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Trigger sprayers and pump tops require more clearance than the bottle body. Send the complete assembled pack dimensions and confirm leakage controls separately; packaging does not replace product closure testing.

Buyer FAQ

Are the spray and candle part of the order?

No. They are reference props. The order covers the custom box, insert and agreed packaging components.

Why is the assembled spray-head size required?

The trigger or pump often extends beyond the bottle body and determines the cavity width, depth and top clearance.

Can this box be made for two spray bottles?

Yes, after the finished bottle dimensions, weights and preferred orientation are supplied.

What should the RFQ include?

Send complete product dimensions, filled weights, quantity, insert preference, artwork, finish references and destination country.
