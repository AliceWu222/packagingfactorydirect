How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Ribbon width, slot position and knot length affect both appearance and opening force. Confirm the packed product, insert height, ribbon sample and retail handling on a physical prototype.

Buyer FAQ

Does the box include jewelry?

No. This page offers custom packaging boxes. Jewelry and gift products are not included.

Can the ribbon color match the box?

A ribbon color can be selected against the paper and artwork, but a physical sample is recommended because textile and paper color appearance differs.

Can an insert be added for earrings or necklaces?

Yes. Supply the product profile, dimensions and retention points so the insert can be developed for the intended item.

What is needed for an RFQ?

Send box size or product dimensions, quantity, insert style, ribbon reference, artwork, finish requirements and destination country.
