How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

The same digital color value will not look identical on every paper. Buyers should approve physical proofs or production samples on each selected substrate before bulk production.

Buyer FAQ

Is this page selling cosmetics or packaging?

Packaging only. The primary product is the mailer box; a paper bag and tissue can be specified as matching packaging components.

Can the box, bag and tissue use one brand color?

Yes, but each substrate needs its own print setup and physical color approval because paper shade and absorbency differ.

Can I order only the mailer box?

Yes. The RFQ can cover the box alone or a coordinated packaging set, subject to the confirmed specification.

What information is needed?

Send box internal size or product layout, packed weight, quantity, bag size if required, tissue size, artwork, color reference and destination.
