How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

A paperboard insert is not suitable for every glass weight or drop condition. Container dimensions, filled weights, stick length and distribution route should be checked before choosing the final insert construction.

Buyer FAQ

Are the candle, diffuser and sticks included?

No. They are reference props. This page offers the custom box, insert and specified packaging components.

When can a paperboard insert be used?

It may suit stable containers and controlled loads, subject to dieline, weight, clearance and sample testing.

Can the sticks have their own compartment?

Yes. Their length, bundle diameter and retention method should be included in the insert layout.

What is needed for a factory quote?
