How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Insert cavities cannot be finalized from bottle volume alone. Send the capped bottle and atomizer dimensions, maximum diameter, base profile and filled weight. Compatibility and transport performance must be confirmed on an approved sample.

Buyer FAQ

Is this listing for perfume or for the packaging box?

It is for the custom packaging box and fitted insert. Bottles and atomizers in the image are neutral reference props and are not included.

What measurements are needed for a perfume insert?

Send the capped bottle height, maximum width and depth or diameter, base profile, filled weight, atomizer size and the preferred display orientation.

Can the box hold more than one fragrance bottle?

Yes, the insert layout can be developed for one bottle, a bottle plus atomizer, or a multi-item fragrance set after dimensions are supplied.

What should be included in the RFQ?

Provide box quantity, product dimensions and weight, insert preference, artwork file, finish references, destination country and target packing method.
