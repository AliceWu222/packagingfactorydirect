How to Specify This Packaging

  1. List every packed item and send its finished dimensions, weight and intended orientation.
  2. Confirm the required box structure, opening direction, insert material and removal method.
  3. Separate artwork layers for print, foil, embossing, debossing and any interior graphics.
  4. State order quantity, destination country, fulfillment method and transport conditions.
  5. Approve the dieline, color proof and physical sample before bulk production.

Important Fit and Production Limitation

Loose satin alone is not a protective insert. The jar, diffuser bottle and reeds need a supporting tray, neck clearance and cap-height check. Fragrance leakage compatibility and shipping tests remain project-specific.

Buyer FAQ

Is the satin itself the product holder?

Not usually. A supportive tray or insert sits below the satin-style surface so the jar and diffuser bottle remain positioned.

Are the candle and diffuser included?

No. They are visual props. The quoted product is the custom box, insert and requested packaging components.

Can the layout hold one candle and two diffusers?

Yes, after all container dimensions, filled weights and display orientations are supplied for dieline development.

What should buyers send for an RFQ?

Send each item’s dimensions and weight, reed length, quantity, box style, insert preference, artwork, finishes and destination country.
