Testing Guide

Packaging Testing Standards: ISTA, Drop & Compression

Test before you ship to avoid damage claims.

Direct answer

Packaging testing verifies protection under real logistics conditions. ISTA procedures (such as ISTA-3A) simulate ecommerce transit; drop tests check impact resistance; compression and edge-crush tests verify stacking strength. Choose tests by product fragility and shipping route.

FAQ

What is ISTA-3A?

A widely used test procedure simulating ecommerce parcel transit, including vibration, drops and compression.

Do I need testing for every product?

Testing matters most for fragile, heavy or high-value products and long shipping routes.

Can the factory run tests?

Some tests can be arranged with testing labs; confirm capability in the RFQ.

How do I know my box passes?

Define pass criteria (no damage, acceptable deflection) before testing, then verify with the test report.
