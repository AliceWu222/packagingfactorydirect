Ribbon, Tissue Paper & Finishing Touches for Gift Boxes

Small details that turn a box into an experience.

Direct answer

Ribbon, tissue paper, stickers and inserts are the finishing touches that make gift packaging memorable. Branded tissue with a printed logo, a silk ribbon and a custom sticker seal are cost-effective ways to elevate perceived value without changing the box structure.

FAQ

Does branded tissue paper cost much?

No, printed tissue is an inexpensive way to add a premium feel to any gift box.

What ribbon is best for gift boxes?

Silk or satin ribbon in brand colors is the classic choice; grosgrain suits a more casual look.

Can I add a custom sticker seal?

Yes, custom die-cut stickers with brand logos or care messages are easy to add.

Are these extras available with my box order?

Yes, ribbon, tissue, stickers and cards can be included in your packaging order from MOQ 500 PCS.
