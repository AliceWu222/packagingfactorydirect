Pet Food Pouch Packaging: Barrier & Print Guide

Protect freshness and stand out on the pet aisle.

Direct answer

Pet food pouches need high barrier against oxygen and moisture to protect freshness and fats, a resealable zipper for convenience, and vivid print for shelf appeal. Common structures are PET/PE for dry treats and MOPP/VMPET/PE or foil laminates for longer shelf life. Confirm food-contact compliance and shelf-life targets in the RFQ.

FAQ

What barrier do pet food pouches need?

Oxygen and moisture barrier protect freshness and prevent rancidity in fats; VMPET or foil layers provide the highest protection.

Are zipper pouches common for pet food?

Yes, resealable zippers are standard for kibble and treats to keep product fresh after opening.

Can pet food pouches be printed with photos?

Yes, gravure or digital print reproduces appetizing product photography well on pouches.

What MOQ applies to pet food pouches?

MOQ 500 PCS applies at Packaging Factory Direct, with samples available first.
