Stand Up Pouch Price Guide

What makes a custom stand up pouch cost more per thousand?

Direct answer

Stand up pouch cost is driven by film structure and barrier level, pouch size, zipper or spout features, print design and quantity. A standard PET/PE pouch is the budget option; high-barrier laminates with VMPET or foil, and added zippers or spouts, raise cost per unit.

Comparison table

Film structureMore layers and barrier films cost moreUse PET/PE for short-shelf-life; add barrier only when needed

RFQ checklist

  • Product type and fill weight
  • Barrier and shelf-life target
  • Zipper, spout, tear notch or hang hole
  • Printing colors and surface finish
  • Quantity and packing method

FAQ

Why do stand up pouch prices vary so much between suppliers?

Differences in film structure, barrier quality, print quality and quantity terms cause large price gaps. Compare quotes on the same structure and specs.

Are zipper pouches much more expensive?

Zippers add a meaningful per-unit cost. They are worthwhile for resealable products like coffee, nuts and pet treats.

Can I get pouches with a spout?

Yes. Spout pouches are available for liquids and are priced higher than flat-top pouches due to the fitment and assembly.
