Color Guide

Pantone & Metallic Inks on Packaging

Brand colors must survive the press, not just the screen.

Direct answer

Pantone spot inks match brand colors precisely; metallic inks add a shiny, reflective finish. Specify Pantone numbers in artwork and approve a physical proof, because screen colors never match print exactly. Metallic inks are a lower-cost alternative to foil stamping for large areas.

FAQ

Why doesn't my screen color match the print?

Screens show RGB; print uses CMYK or Pantone inks. Always approve a physical proof under normal light.

Are metallic inks cheaper than foil?

Metallic inks are usually cheaper than foil stamping, especially for large areas, but the finish is less mirror-like.

Can Pantone colors be printed on kraft?

Yes, but the brown base changes the appearance. A coated liner or white basecoat is needed for true color.

Do you provide color proofs?

Yes, digital and physical proofs are part of the standard approval process.
