Printing Comparison

Offset vs Digital Printing for Packaging

Two printing paths for custom boxes, with different trade-offs.

Direct answer

Digital printing has no plate cost, suits 500-2,000 pcs and fast turnaround; offset printing delivers superior color consistency and lower unit cost from roughly 1,000-2,000 pcs upward. For most custom packaging runs above 1,000 pcs, offset is the better value; digital is ideal for samples and short runs.

FAQ

Which printing is cheaper for 500 pcs?

Digital. Without plate costs, short runs are far cheaper than offset at 500 pcs.

When does offset become cheaper?

Depending on plate cost and run length, offset usually wins from about 1,000-2,000 pcs and above.

Is digital printing good quality for packaging?

Modern digital print is high quality and fine for most brand packaging, especially with lamination.

Can I mix digital samples and offset production?

Yes. Many brands approve digital samples, then switch to offset for the production run.
