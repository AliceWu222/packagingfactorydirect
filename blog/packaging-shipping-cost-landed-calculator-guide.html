Packaging Shipping Cost & Landed Cost Guide

How do you calculate the real landed cost of imported packaging?

Direct answer

Landed cost equals product price plus freight, insurance, customs duties, and local delivery. Freight for packaging is usually charged by dimensional weight, so collapsible structures like folding cartons and mailer boxes ship much cheaper than rigid or magnetic boxes of the same volume.

FAQ

Why does freight cost more than the boxes sometimes?

Rigid and magnetic boxes are bulky and non-collapsible, so dimensional weight drives freight above the product cost for small orders. Collapsible structures reduce this.

How can I reduce packaging shipping cost?

Choose collapsible structures, reduce inner packaging, consolidate SKUs into one container, and compare FOB vs CIF quotes.

What is dimensional weight?

Freight carriers charge by volume (length x width x height / divisor) when it exceeds actual weight. Packaging is often billed this way.
