Packaging Production Lead Times: Boxes & Pouches

Plan production time backwards from your launch date.

Direct answer

Packaging lead time covers design and dieline, sampling, approval, tooling, production, QC and shipping. Simple folding cartons are fastest; rigid boxes and gravure pouches take longer. Add shipping time, which varies by route and mode.

FAQ

How long does a custom box order take?

Typically a few weeks from approved artwork to production completion, plus shipping time.

Do pouches take longer than boxes?

Gravure pouches need cylinder engraving, adding time. Digital-printed pouches are faster for short runs.

Can I rush a packaging order?

Expedited production may be possible for an extra charge; confirm with the factory.

How much shipping time should I add?

Air freight is days to a week; sea freight is weeks. Confirm the route with your forwarder.
