Custom Packaging Budget Guide

How should a small brand budget for custom packaging?

Direct answer

Budget packaging by structure, quantity and finish. Folding cartons and mailer boxes give the lowest cost per unit, while rigid and magnetic boxes cost several times more. Include tooling, samples, freight and duties in the budget, not just the unit price.

FAQ

What is the cheapest custom packaging for a new brand?

Folding cartons or mailer boxes at MOQ 500 PCS with 1-2 color print give the lowest entry cost for a new brand.

How much should packaging cost as a share of product price?

Most brands target packaging at roughly 5-15% of retail price, with premium SKUs allowed more for unboxing value.

Are there hidden costs in packaging budgets?

Yes. Tooling, samples, freight by dimensional weight, duties and storage are common items buyers forget until the first order.

Should I spend more on packaging for a premium product?

For premium SKUs, rigid or magnetic boxes can raise perceived value and support higher pricing, but reserve them for hero products only.
