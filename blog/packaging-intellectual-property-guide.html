Protecting Your Design & IP with Packaging Factories

Protect your design before sharing it.

Direct answer

Protect packaging IP with a signed NDA, written die and mold ownership, and exclusivity terms where needed. Register trademarks and design rights in your key markets, and watermark artwork before mass sharing. Choose a factory with a clean IP record.

FAQ

Do factories copy designs?

Reputable factories protect customer IP. Choose a supplier with a clean record and signed agreements.

Who owns the dies after production?

Whoever pays for the dies owns them, per the agreement. Confirm ownership and storage terms in writing.

Can I stop a factory from reselling my design?

An exclusivity clause plus registered design rights are the strongest protections. Confirm both with your supplier and legal advisor.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, Packaging Factory Direct signs NDAs and respects customer design ownership.
