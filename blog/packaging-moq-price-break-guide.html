How do quantity tiers change your packaging unit price?

Direct answer

Packaging unit price falls in steps as quantity rises, because setup, plate/die costs and machine changeover are spread over more units. The largest savings usually appear between 500 and 3,000 pcs; beyond that, savings per step get smaller. Plan orders around tier thresholds rather than arbitrary quantities.

FAQ

Why does MOQ 500 cost so much more per unit than 5,000?

Setup, dies, plates and machine time are fixed costs spread over units. At 500 pcs they weigh heavily; at 5,000 they become small.

Is it cheaper to order multiple SKUs at once?

Usually yes, because setup and logistics are shared. Some factories offer combined quantity pricing across SKUs.

Can tooling be reused for reorders?

Yes. Dies, plates and cylinders are stored and reused, so reorders skip tooling cost and often reach lower unit prices faster.

Should I order at exactly the tier boundary?

Ask for quotes at several quantities, such as 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000, and choose the tier where the price drop justifies the extra inventory.
