Packaging Print Cost Guide: Gravure vs Digital vs Flexo

How do printing methods change the cost of your packaging?

Direct answer

Printing method choice is a quantity decision. Digital printing suits small batches with no plate cost; flexo suits medium runs; gravure has the highest plate cost but the lowest unit cost at large volumes. Color count and artwork complexity also drive cost across all methods.

RFQ checklist

  • Order quantity and expected reorders
  • Number of colors and gradients
  • Surface finish (lamination, gloss, matte)
  • Printing substrate (paper, film, kraft)
  • Sample need before mass production

FAQ

Which printing method is cheapest for small quantities?

Digital printing has no plate cost, making it the cheapest option for 500-2,000 pcs and for samples.

When should I switch to gravure printing?

At roughly 50,000+ pcs, gravure's high cylinder cost is amortized and unit cost becomes the lowest, especially for film pouches.

Does color count affect packaging print cost?

Yes. Each additional color adds cost in flexo and gravure. Digital printing charges are less color-sensitive.
