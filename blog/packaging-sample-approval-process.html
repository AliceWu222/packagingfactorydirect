Sample Guide

Packaging Sample Approval: What to Check

Approve the sample, not the promise.

Direct answer

Check the sample against specifications: size and fit, color accuracy, finish, structure, insert and print sharpness. Test the sample with your product and, if needed, in transit. Approve in writing with clear notes so mass production matches the approved sample.

Comparison table

CheckHow to verifyCommon issue
Size and fitMeasure and test with productTight or loose fit
ColorCompare to Pantone/proofColor shift
FinishTouch and light checkUneven lamination
StructureAssemble and open/closeMisaligned creases
InsertPlace product in cavityImproper fit

RFQ checklist

  • Measure the sample against spec
  • Test color against the proof
  • Assemble and test the structure
  • Test the insert with your product
  • Approve in writing with notes

FAQ

How long do samples take?

Sample lead time varies by structure; digital print samples are fastest, while rigid boxes and gravure pouches take longer.

Can I request changes after seeing the sample?

Yes, revisions are normal. Note changes clearly so the revised sample matches expectations.

Is the sample representative of mass production?

Generally yes, but final production may have minor variations. Confirm tolerances with the factory.

Do samples cost extra?

Sample policy varies; confirm cost and shipping terms in the RFQ.
