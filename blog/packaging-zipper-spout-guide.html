Pouch Features Guide

Zippers, Spouts & Closures for Pouches

Reseal and pour features decide daily usability.

Direct answer

Zippers make pouches resealable; spouts enable pouring liquids and granules; tear notches and hang holes support convenience and display. Each feature adds material and assembly cost, so choose only the closures your product actually needs.

Comparison table

FeatureUseCost impact
ZipperResealable snacks, coffee, pet foodMedium
SpoutLiquids, sauces, powdersHigh
Tear notchEasy openLow
Hang holeRetail displayLow
Slider zipperEasy close, premium feelHigh

RFQ checklist

  • Reseal requirement after opening
  • Pouring or dispensing need
  • Display (hang hole) requirement
  • Feature compatibility with filling line
  • Cost per unit impact

FAQ

Are zipper pouches worth the extra cost?

For products consumed over time, like coffee and snacks, zippers preserve freshness and improve usability.

What pouches need spouts?

Liquids, sauces, beverages and granule products benefit from spouts for clean pouring.

Can I add both zipper and spout?

Yes, some pouch designs combine them, though cost and assembly complexity increase.

Do features affect the filling line?

Yes, zippers and spouts must match your filling equipment. Confirm compatibility before production.
