Small Batch Guide

Small Batch Packaging (500 PCS) Strategies

Launch and test packaging without overcommitting.

Direct answer

Small batches at MOQ 500 PCS work well with digital printing, standard structures and simpler finishes. They suit market tests, launch batches and short-run products. Keep tooling shared and design simple to control the higher per-unit cost of small runs.

RFQ checklist

  • Print method for the volume

  • Finish scope

FAQ

Is 500 pcs enough for a market test?

Yes, 500 pcs is enough to test structure, finish and buyer response before scaling.

How do I keep small batch cost down?

Use digital printing, standard structures and limited finishes to control per-unit cost.

Can I reorder the same design easily?

Yes, reorders reuse artwork and any tooling, and per-unit cost drops as quantity grows.

What MOQ applies?

MOQ 500 PCS applies across structures at Packaging Factory Direct.
