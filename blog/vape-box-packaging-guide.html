FAQ

What is a child-resistant box?

A carton or pouch that passes a child-resistant opening test while remaining senior-friendly. Confirm the standard required by your market.

Do I need special warning labels?

Regulated products typically require specific warning text and symbols. Confirm layout with your compliance team before print.

Can you print the required QR or track codes?

Yes, including serialized codes where required by regulation.

What MOQ applies to regulated packaging?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples for CR testing available.
