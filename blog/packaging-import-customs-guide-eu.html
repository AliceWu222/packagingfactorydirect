EU Packaging Import: CE, REACH & EPR Basics

European rules are stricter than most markets.

Direct answer

EU imports of packaging must consider REACH chemical compliance, food-contact rules for food packaging, and packaging EPR (extended producer responsibility) obligations. Confirm the applicable regulations with your compliance advisor, as rules vary by country and product type.

FAQ

Does packaging need CE marking?

CE marking applies to certain products, not general packaging. Confirm with your compliance advisor whether your product category requires it.

What is packaging EPR?

Extended producer responsibility requires importers to register packaging and pay fees for collection and recycling in the destination country.

Do I need REACH documents?

REACH compliance declarations help verify restricted substances are not present. Request them from your supplier.

Can you provide EU compliance documents?

We can provide material declarations and support documents for your compliance process.
