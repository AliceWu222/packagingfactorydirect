Finish Guide

Matte vs Glossy Lamination for Packaging

The finish layer defines how the box feels and looks.

Direct answer

Matte lamination gives a soft, elegant, fingerprint-resistant surface; glossy lamination makes colors pop with a bright shine and adds durability. Choose matte for premium and minimal brands, glossy for vibrant retail products, and consider matte-plus-spot-UV for both premium feel and highlights.

FAQ

Which lamination is more durable?

Glossy lamination is generally more scuff-resistant; matte can show scuffs more easily but looks more premium.

Can I combine matte and spot UV?

Yes, matte base with spot UV highlights is the most popular premium combination.

Does lamination add cost?

A small per-unit cost, well justified by the premium look and print protection.

Can soft-touch lamination be used on folding cartons?

Yes, soft-touch works on cartons and is a signature finish in cosmetics packaging.
