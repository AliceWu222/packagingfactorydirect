Importing Packaging to the USA: Duties & Documents

Know the documents and duties before your boxes land.

Direct answer

Importing packaging to the USA needs correct HS codes, commercial documents and, for food-contact products, FDA compliance. Duties depend on material and classification. Work with a licensed customs broker and confirm the HS code with your forwarder before shipping.

FAQ

What HS code applies to paper boxes?

Boxes of paper or paperboard typically fall under heading 4819. Confirm the exact code with your broker, as it affects the duty rate.

Do I need FDA compliance for packaging?

Food-contact packaging should meet FDA requirements. Confirm with your supplier and import documents.

Who pays duties on imports?

The importer of record pays duties and taxes. Confirm terms (DDP, DDU) with your supplier.
