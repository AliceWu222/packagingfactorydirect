Cosmetic Packaging Boxes: Factory Guide for Brands

What beauty brands should specify when sourcing packaging boxes.

Direct answer

Cosmetic boxes are chosen for premium feel, precise print and strong product protection. Folding cartons cover most skincare SKUs; magnetic and rigid boxes carry hero products and gift sets. Specify finishes, insert fit and print accuracy early, and confirm MOQ 500 PCS and sample policy with the factory.

RFQ checklist

  • Product size and insert fit
  • Box structure and board
  • Finishes and brand colors
  • MOQ and lead time
  • Sample and proof process

FAQ

What is the standard MOQ for cosmetic boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies at Packaging Factory Direct, with samples available before mass production.

What finishes make cosmetic boxes look premium?

Matte lamination with spot UV or gold foil, plus embossing, are the most effective premium finishes for beauty packaging.

How long does a cosmetic box order take?

Typical production is a few weeks after sample approval; confirm the exact lead time in your RFQ.

Can you match my brand colors exactly?

Yes. Pantone color matching with a physical proof is part of the standard process.
