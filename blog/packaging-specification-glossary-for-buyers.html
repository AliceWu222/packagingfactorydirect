Packaging Specification Glossary for B2B Buyers

Use these definitions to remove ambiguity before a custom packaging quote. A term alone is rarely enough: record the unit, test method, conditions, tolerance, approved reference and who is responsible for verification.

Direct answer: what is a packaging specification?

A packaging specification is the controlled set of dimensions, structure, material or performance requirements, graphics, finishes, tolerances, approval evidence, packing instructions and delivery assumptions used to quote, sample, manufacture and inspect a package. It should distinguish fixed requirements from supplier recommendations and record every approved revision.

Dimensions, artwork and structure

TermBuyer-ready definitionWhat to state in an RFQ
Internal dimensionsThe usable space inside an assembled package.Length × width × depth, measurement direction, clearance and the actual packed product.
External dimensionsThe maximum outside size of the finished or packed package.Whether measured empty, assembled or fully packed; include closures and projections.
DielineA production drawing showing cut, crease, perforation, glue, window and artwork-control areas.Scale, units, revision, structural owner, bleed, safe area and print-free technical zones.
Bleed / trim / safe areaBleed extends artwork beyond the final cut; trim is the intended cut; the safe area keeps critical content away from variable edges.Use the converter's approved values and keep the approved dieline as a locked layer.
ToleranceThe allowed variation around a nominal value.State a measurable range, measurement method, sampling plan and disposition rule rather than writing “industry standard.”
InsertA fitted component that locates or protects the product inside the outer pack.Product dimensions and weight, cavity orientation, retention need, contact restrictions and drop/shipping use.

For a practical measurement workflow, use How to Measure a Product for a Custom Box. For a comparable quote brief, use the custom packaging RFQ guide.

Paper, board and corrugated terms

TermMeaningProcurement caution
GSM / grammageMass per unit area, normally expressed in grams per square metre. ISO 536 covers determination of paper and board grammage.GSM does not uniquely determine thickness, density, stiffness, fiber composition or surface treatment.
Caliper / pt / mmCaliper is material thickness. ISO 534 addresses thickness, density and specific volume of paper and board.State the unit and measurement method. Do not convert GSM to thickness with one universal factor.
FluteThe corrugated medium profile between liners.Name the board construction, liner/medium basis or grade and any required compression or print performance.
ECTEdge Crush Test, a measure related to corrugated board edgewise compression.Do not treat an ECT value as a complete shipping-performance guarantee; package design, humidity, stacking and distribution still matter.
BCTBox Compression Test on an assembled container.Define sample conditioning, test method, load direction and acceptance criteria.
Mullen / burstA pressure-to-rupture measurement used for some paper and corrugated specifications.It is not interchangeable with ECT or a transit-test result.

Flexible packaging and seal terms

TermMeaningWhat makes the number comparable
OTROxygen transmission rate. ASTM D3985 describes a coulometric method for plastic film and sheeting.Test method, temperature, relative humidity, specimen orientation, units and whether the figure applies to film or a finished package.
WVTRWater vapor transmission rate. ASTM F1249 describes a modulated-infrared method for plastic film and sheeting.Test method and conditions must accompany the result; a bare “high barrier” claim is not a specification.
Seal initiation / seal windowThe temperature region in which a sealing layer develops an acceptable seal under defined time and pressure.Machine type, jaw temperature, dwell, pressure, contamination risk and required seal-strength method.
COFCoefficient of friction between surfaces.Identify static or kinetic COF, surface-to-surface orientation, method and converting/packing-machine need.
Laminate structureThe ordered material layers and their thicknesses or basis weights.Write the complete layer order, total thickness, adhesive/coating assumptions and product-contact side.

Barrier selection must start with the product, target shelf life, filling and sealing process, storage and route. The OTR/WVTR RFQ guide explains why values without conditions cannot be compared.

Print, color and finishing terms

TermMeaningApproval evidence
CMYK process colorColor built from cyan, magenta, yellow and black process inks.Approved contract proof or production reference appropriate to the print process and substrate.
Spot colorA separately specified ink color rather than a CMYK build.Named color system/reference, substrate, coating and acceptable measurement or visual conditions.
ΔEA calculated color difference under a named color-difference formula and measurement setup.Formula, illuminant, observer, instrument geometry, substrate, measurement mode and acceptance rule.
Foil / emboss / deboss / spot UVSeparate decorative conversion operations: transferred foil, raised relief, recessed relief and localized coating.Vector artwork layer, target area, material reference, tooling/proof scope and tolerance to print or cut.

ISO 12647-2 covers process control for offset lithographic production, but a standard number alone does not prove a printer's result. Define the exact process and approval evidence. See the packaging color matching guide.

Logistics, testing and compliance language

  • Transit test: a controlled procedure selected for a stated distribution system. ISTA explains that its 1-Series are screening tests while 3-Series are general simulations. Naming a procedure does not prove a package passed it.
  • Dimensional weight: carrier billing based on parcel volume under current service rules. Record the final export-carton outside dimensions; do not assume one universal divisor.
  • Food contact: suitability depends on the material, food, contact conditions and destination rules. FDA and the European Commission publish separate food-contact frameworks; “food grade” without evidence tied to the use case is incomplete.
  • Incoterms rule and named place: the selected rule, edition and exact named place or port. It does not replace the product specification, sales contract or destination compliance review.

Buyer FAQ

Are GSM and thickness interchangeable?

No. GSM is grammage; caliper is thickness. The same grammage can produce different thickness, density, stiffness and converting behavior.

Can OTR or WVTR be compared without conditions?

No. Record the method, temperature, relative humidity, orientation and units. Different methods or conditions can produce non-comparable values.

What belongs on a dieline?

Identify cut, crease, perforation, glue, window, bleed, safe areas, dimensions, scale, version and print-free technical zones.

Does this glossary replace engineering or compliance review?

No. It helps buyers write comparable RFQs. Product protection and destination-market compliance require project-specific evidence and qualified review.
