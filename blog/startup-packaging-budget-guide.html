Startup Guide

Startup Packaging on a Budget

Packaging that looks premium without a premium budget.

Direct answer

Startups can get premium-looking packaging on a budget by choosing cost-efficient structures, digital printing, limited finishes and smart design. Spend the budget on the hero SKU and keep secondary SKUs simple.

FAQ

How can a startup get premium packaging cheaply?

Use folding cartons with matte lamination, digital printing and one signature finish like spot UV or foil on the logo.

What is the minimum order for startups?

MOQ 500 PCS lets startups test packaging without large inventory risk.

Should I spend more on hero products?

Yes, concentrate the premium budget on hero SKUs and keep supporting SKUs simple.

Can I upgrade packaging later?

Yes, start with a budget structure and upgrade finishes or structures as sales grow.
