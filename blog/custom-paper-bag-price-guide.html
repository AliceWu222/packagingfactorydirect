Custom Paper Bag Price Guide

What decides the price of custom paper bags and gift bags?

Direct answer

Paper bag pricing depends on paper type and GSM, bag size, handle style, printing, bottom style and quantity. Kraft paper bags are the most economical; coated or laminated bags with rope handles cost more. Handles and reinforcement add the most labor and material cost.

FAQ

What is the cheapest paper bag option?

A kraft paper bag with a twisted paper handle and 1-2 color print is the most economical custom option.

Do paper bags cost more than plastic bags?

Paper bags typically cost more per unit than plastic, but are preferred by many retail brands for sustainability positioning and shelf appeal.

Can paper bags be fully custom in size?

Yes. Bags are made to your size and load requirement from MOQ 500 PCS at Packaging Factory Direct.

What handle is best for heavy shopping bags?

Rope handles with reinforced punch holes are best for heavy loads; twisted paper handles suit light to medium retail use.
