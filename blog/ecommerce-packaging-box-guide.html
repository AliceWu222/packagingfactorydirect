Ecommerce Packaging: Boxes That Convert

Packaging is part of the online purchase experience.

Direct answer

Great ecommerce packaging protects the product, fits the shipping box, and creates a branded unboxing moment. Right-size boxes reduce void fill and freight; inside print, tissue and a thank-you card lift perceived value. Test your box in transit before committing.

FAQ

What box is best for ecommerce?

Mailer boxes balance protection, freight and branding; folding cartons suit products that need an outer shipper.

How do I reduce ecommerce shipping costs?

Right-size boxes, reduce void fill, and use collapsible structures to cut dimensional weight.

Does unboxing really matter?

Yes, a memorable unboxing increases social sharing and repeat purchases, especially for DTC brands.

What MOQ applies to ecommerce boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples and transit testing available.
