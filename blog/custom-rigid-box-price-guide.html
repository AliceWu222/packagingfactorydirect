Custom Rigid Box Price Guide

What actually drives the unit price of a custom rigid box?

Direct answer

Rigid box unit price is driven by size, greyboard thickness, wrapping material, finishes, insert type, order quantity and tooling. Because rigid boxes are assembled by hand, labor is a larger share of cost than with folding cartons, so structure and quantity planning matter more.

RFQ checklist

  • Product dimensions and target box inner size
  • Greyboard thickness and wrap material
  • Finishes: foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, matte lamination
  • Insert type and product protection level
  • Target quantity per order and annual volume
  • Artwork format and dieline requirement

FAQ

Why are rigid boxes more expensive than folding cartons?

Rigid boxes use thicker greyboard, are wrapped and assembled by hand, and are not collapsible, so material, labor and shipping cost per unit are higher.

Can I get a lower rigid box price with a larger MOQ?

Yes. Rigid box pricing drops noticeably at higher quantities because setup, tooling and labor are spread over more units. Share your annual volume in the RFQ.

Do I need tooling for a rigid box?

Most rigid boxes need a one-time die or assembly fixture. The cost is one-off and is often amortized into the unit price across the order.

Can small quantities still get factory direct pricing?

Yes. Packaging Factory Direct starts rigid box orders from MOQ 500 PCS, which suits market tests and launch batches.
