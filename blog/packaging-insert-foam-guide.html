Insert Guide

Packaging Inserts & Foam: EVA, Sponge, Molded Pulp

The insert protects the product and completes the unboxing.

Direct answer

Inserts hold products securely in boxes. EVA and sponge foams give precise custom cavities for fragile or premium items; molded pulp is a sustainable, lower-cost option for many products. Choose by product weight, fragility, presentation level and sustainability goals.

FAQ

Which insert protects glass bottles best?

EVA foam with a molded cavity protects glass best; sponge is a lighter, cheaper alternative for smaller items.

Are molded pulp inserts eco-friendly?

Yes, molded pulp is recyclable and biodegradable, and it is a growing choice for sustainable brands.

Can inserts be custom shaped?

Yes, EVA and sponge are cut or molded to any cavity shape for your product.

Do inserts add much cost?

Paperboard trays add little; EVA and molded pulp add more. Include insert cost in the unit price comparison.
