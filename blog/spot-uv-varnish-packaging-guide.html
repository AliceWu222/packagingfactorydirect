Finish Guide

Spot UV & Varnish on Packaging Boxes

Contrast in finish creates the premium look.

Direct answer

Spot UV adds a glossy, raised highlight on selected areas over a matte surface, creating a premium tactile contrast. Varnish protects the print and controls gloss. Use spot UV on logos, product names or patterns, and keep the rest matte for the strongest effect.

FAQ

Is spot UV worth the cost?

Yes, spot UV is one of the most cost-effective premium finishes and adds clear perceived value on boxes.

Can spot UV be applied over matte lamination?

Yes, that is the classic combination: matte base with glossy spot UV highlights.

Does spot UV work on kraft?

It works, but the contrast is subtler on brown kraft. Test a sample first.

How do I specify the spot UV area?

Provide a separate spot-UV layer in your artwork or mark the areas clearly for the factory.
