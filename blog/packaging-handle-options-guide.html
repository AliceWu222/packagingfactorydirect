Handle Guide

Handles for Paper Bags: Rope, Ribbon & Die-Cut

The handle sets the feel of a bag in hand.

Direct answer

Handle choice affects cost, strength and brand feel. Twisted paper handles are economical and strong; flat paper handles are budget; rope handles feel premium and carry heavy loads; ribbon handles suit luxury gift bags; die-cut handles are cheapest but carry less.

Comparison table

Handle typeCostBest use
Twisted paperLowRetail, medium loads
Flat paperLowestBudget retail bags
RopeMediumHeavy shopping, premium
RibbonMedium-highLuxury gift bags

RFQ checklist

  • Load weight and handle strength
  • Brand style: premium or budget
  • Handle material and color
  • Reinforcement for heavy loads
  • Quantity

FAQ

Which handle is strongest?

Rope handles with reinforced punch holes carry the heaviest loads; twisted paper is strong for typical retail use.

What handle is cheapest?

Die-cut handles (cut from the bag itself) cost the least; flat paper handles are the cheapest added handle.

Can handles match my brand colors?

Yes, handles can be specified in brand colors or custom-printed.

Do handles need reinforcement for heavy items?

Yes, heavier loads need reinforced punch holes or patches to prevent tearing.
