White Label vs Custom Packaging Suppliers

Speed today or full control tomorrow?

Direct answer

White label packaging uses existing structures with your branding, offering speed and lower cost; custom packaging builds structures to your product and brand, offering full control and differentiation. Choose white label for speed to market, custom for brand uniqueness.

FAQ

What is white label packaging?

Packaging using the supplier's existing structures with your branding applied, without custom structure development.

Can I move from white label to custom later?

Yes, many brands start white label and move to custom structures as volume and budget grow.

Which is cheaper?

White label is usually cheaper because tooling and structures are shared across customers.

Do you offer both?

Yes, Packaging Factory Direct supports both white label-style branding and full custom structures.
