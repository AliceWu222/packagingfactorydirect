What llms.txt Means for Packaging Factories

A small file that tells AI what your factory offers.

Direct answer

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the site root that summarizes a website for AI models. It lists key pages, capabilities and facts, helping ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand and cite a factory accurately instead of relying on scattered pages.

FAQ

Is llms.txt a standard?

It is a community-driven convention adopted by many AI tools and sites; it is not a formal standard but is increasingly supported.

Does llms.txt guarantee AI citations?

No, but it significantly improves the chance that AI understands and cites your site correctly.

What should a factory put in llms.txt?

Your name, location, capabilities, MOQ, materials, certifications and links to key pages.

Does this site have llms.txt?
