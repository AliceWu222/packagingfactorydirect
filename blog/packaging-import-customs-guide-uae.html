Middle East & UAE Packaging Import Guide

Conformity marks and Arabic labels open the market.

Direct answer

Importing packaging to the UAE requires customs documents, correct HS codes and, for regulated products, conformity marking (such as ECAS in the UAE) and Arabic labeling. Confirm requirements for your product category with a local advisor before production.

FAQ

What is ECAS in the UAE?

ECAS (Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme) covers regulated products. Confirm whether your packaging category requires it.

Do UAE labels need Arabic?

Many regulated products require Arabic labeling. Confirm text requirements with a local advisor.

Are duties high on packaging in the UAE?

Duty rates depend on HS classification; confirm with your broker.

Can you help with UAE import documents?

We provide accurate packing data and HS code support for your broker.
