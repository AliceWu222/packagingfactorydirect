Gift Box Price Comparison: Rigid vs Magnetic vs Mailer

Which gift box structure fits your budget and brand?

Direct answer

Mailer boxes are the lowest-cost structure, rigid boxes the highest, and magnetic rigid boxes sit between when produced in quantity. The right choice depends on product value, unboxing experience and order volume. Rigid and magnetic boxes are hand-assembled and non-collapsible, which also raises freight cost.

RFQ checklist

FAQ

Which gift box is cheapest to ship?

Mailer boxes and folding cartons are collapsible, so they ship flat and cost far less in freight than rigid or magnetic boxes.

Are magnetic boxes worth the higher price?

For premium products, the magnetic close creates a strong unboxing experience that supports higher retail pricing, which can offset the higher unit cost.

Can I test a premium box at low quantity?

Yes. MOQ 500 PCS applies across gift box structures, so you can market-test a magnetic or rigid box before scaling.

Do rigid boxes need tooling?

Most rigid and magnetic boxes need one-time assembly tooling or dies, amortized across the order.
