Sustainability Guide

Packaging Sustainability Certifications: FSC & More

Back eco claims with verifiable certificates.

Direct answer

Sustainability claims need proof. FSC certifies responsible forest sourcing; recycled content certificates verify post-consumer material; compostable standards (such as TUV/BPI) verify biodegradability. Request certificates and understand which claims you can legally make in your market.

FAQ

What is FSC certification?

FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certifies that paper and wood products come from responsibly managed forests.

Can I claim my packaging is compostable?

Only with valid compostable certification and confirmation of disposal routes in your market.

Are recycled-content claims regulated?

Yes, many markets regulate recycling claims. Use certified percentages and accurate wording.

Can you provide sustainability documents?

Yes, request certificates and material declarations in your RFQ.
