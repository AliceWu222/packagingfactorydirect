Comparing Packaging Quotes: What Buyers Miss

The lowest unit price is rarely the cheapest total cost.

Direct answer

Compare quotes on total landed cost: unit price plus tooling, samples, freight, duties and lead time, with the same specs. Buyers often miss tooling ownership, freight by dimensional weight, sample costs and quality differences between low and high quotes.

RFQ checklist

  • Send identical specs to all suppliers
  • Request tooling and sample costs
  • Compare total landed cost
  • Order samples from top quotes
  • Check lead time and reliability

FAQ

Why are quotes so different for the same product?

Differences in material, finish, MOQ terms and freight assumptions cause gaps. Standardize specs and compare like for like.

What should I compare beyond unit price?

Tooling, samples, freight, duties, lead time, quality and communication reliability.

Should I choose the cheapest quote?

Not always. Verify material and finish quality with samples before deciding.

Can you provide a comparable quote?

Yes, Packaging Factory Direct quotes against your complete spec with all costs itemized.
