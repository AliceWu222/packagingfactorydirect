FAQ

How much packaging should I keep in stock?

Enough to cover lead time plus safety stock, typically 1-2 months of forecast sales.

When should I reorder?

Reorder when remaining stock covers the full lead time plus your safety buffer.

How do I reduce packaging stock costs?

Standardize structures, order at price tiers and consolidate SKUs.

Can you help with reorder planning?

Yes, we provide lead times and tier pricing to support your reorder planning.
