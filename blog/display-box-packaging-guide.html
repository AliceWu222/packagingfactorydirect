Display Guide

Counter Display Boxes for Retail

Point-of-sale packaging that sells by itself.

Direct answer

Counter display boxes present products at the point of sale to drive impulse purchases. Design for product visibility, easy restock, stability and strong header branding. Corrugated displays are economical; printed and shaped displays suit premium launches.

FAQ

What is a counter display box?

A small display unit placed at checkout or counter that presents products for impulse purchase.

How many products fit in a display?

It depends on product size and pack layout; design the grid for easy restocking and stability.

Can displays be printed with branding?

Yes, printed displays with header branding and product messaging are standard.

What MOQ applies to display boxes?
