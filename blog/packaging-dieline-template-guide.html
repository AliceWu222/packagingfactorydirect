Packaging Dieline Templates: How to Prepare Artwork

Good dielines prevent costly print mistakes.

Direct answer

A dieline is the flat layout of a box or bag showing cut, crease and fold lines. Prepare artwork on the factory dieline with bleed, safe zones and correct colors, and export a high-resolution PDF. Factories provide dielines for your exact structure before design begins.

Comparison table

LayerPurposeSpecification
BleedPrevents white edges after cutting2-3mm beyond cut line
Cut lineWhere the box is cutFollow the dieline exactly
Fold/crease lineWhere the box bendsKeep artwork clear
Safe zoneKeeps text and logos inside3-5mm from cut line
ColorsMatch brandPantone or CMYK defined

RFQ checklist

  • Request the official dieline from the factory
  • Confirm bleed and safe zone settings
  • Check fold lines do not cross text
  • Define Pantone or CMYK colors
  • Export PDF with embedded fonts and images

FAQ

Can I design without a dieline?

Always request the dieline first. Designing without it risks misplaced graphics and unusable artwork.

What file format do factories need?

High-resolution PDF with embedded fonts, plus the original editable file (AI, CDR or PSD) when possible.

What is the safe zone?

The area inside the cut line where text and logos must stay to avoid being trimmed during cutting.

Do you provide dielines for custom structures?

Yes. Packaging Factory Direct provides dielines for your exact box or bag structure before design.
