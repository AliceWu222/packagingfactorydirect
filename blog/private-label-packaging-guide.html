FAQ

What is private label packaging?

Packaging produced by a factory under your brand and specifications, for retailers or your own products.

Can I keep the same structure across products?

Yes, a family of sizes on one structure keeps tooling and costs down.

Who owns the packaging design?

You own the design and, per agreement, the tooling. Sign agreements to protect your IP.

What MOQ applies?

MOQ 500 PCS applies per design at Packaging Factory Direct.
