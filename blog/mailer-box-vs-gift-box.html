Mailer Box vs Gift Box for Ecommerce Unboxing

Shipment protection and brand moment in one box?

Direct answer

Mailer boxes are strong, collapsible corrugated shippers with printed branding and are the default for ecommerce; gift boxes are rigid or carton boxes designed for presentation, often shipped inside a mailer. Many brands use a printed mailer for daily orders and a gift box for gifting and premium SKUs.

FAQ

Can a mailer box look premium?

Yes. Printed mailer boxes with inside print, custom size and good corrugated surface can create a strong unboxing moment at low cost.

Should I ship a gift box inside a mailer?

For fragile or premium rigid gift boxes, an outer mailer protects the box during transit. The recipient opens the mailer first, then the gift box.

Which is cheaper for ecommerce?

Mailer boxes are cheaper per unit and ship flat, making them the economical default for most online orders.

Can both be custom printed?

Yes, both are fully customizable in size, print and structure from MOQ 500 PCS.
