Direct answer

FAQ

What box is best for a corporate gift set?

Magnetic rigid boxes with a custom insert present multiple items elegantly and feel premium.

Can I personalize boxes per client?

Yes, client names and messages can be printed or added with cards for key accounts.

How do I package different items together?

Custom foam or paper inserts with cavities hold each item securely in one presentation.

What MOQ applies?

MOQ 500 PCS applies; smaller premium orders can be discussed with the factory.
