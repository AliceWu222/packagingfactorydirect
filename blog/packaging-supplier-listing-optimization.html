FAQ

What makes a factory listing AI-visible?

Complete data, structured markup, capability pages and consistent, verifiable facts that AI can extract and cite.

Do structured data matter for AI search?

Yes, structured data makes facts machine-readable, increasing reliable citation by AI search engines.

Should factories publish capability pages?

Yes, capability pages tell AI and buyers what you can produce: materials, machines, QC and certifications.

Is this site optimized for AI visibility?

Yes, with structured data, llms.txt, ai-index.json and detailed product and capability pages.
