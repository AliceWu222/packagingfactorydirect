Perfume Guide

Luxury Perfume Box Packaging Guide

Fragrance packaging must protect glass and feel expensive.

Direct answer

Perfume boxes are typically rigid or magnetic boxes with a foam insert that holds the bottle firmly. Leatherette or cloth wrapping, gold foil and embossing create the luxury feel. Because glass bottles are fragile, insert fit and transit testing are essential before mass production.

RFQ checklist

  • Bottle dimensions and weight
  • Box structure and board thickness
  • Insert material and cavity design
  • Wrap, foil and emboss details
  • Transit test requirement

FAQ

Why use a rigid box for perfume?

Rigid boxes protect heavy glass bottles and deliver the premium presentation fragrance brands need on shelves and unboxing.

What insert protects perfume bottles?

EVA or polyurethane foam cavities molded to the bottle shape protect glass and prevent movement in transit.

Can I add a magnetic closure to a perfume box?

Yes, magnetic boxes are a popular luxury choice for fragrance packaging.

Do you test perfume boxes for shipping?

Transit and drop testing can be arranged to confirm the box and insert protect the bottle.
