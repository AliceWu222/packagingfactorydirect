Window Guide

Window Film for Product Boxes

Show the product while keeping it protected.

Direct answer

Window patches let customers see the product through clear film. PET film is durable and food-safe; cellophane is biodegradable; anti-fog film suits chilled or fresh products. Choose film by product type, food contact and display need.

FAQ

Is window film food-safe?

PET and cellophane window film are available food-grade. Confirm compliance in the RFQ.

Can windows be any shape?

Yes, windows are die-cut to your shape: circles, ovals, cutouts or full panels.

Does a window add much cost?

A window adds film material and an application step, a modest increase over a plain box.

Which film stays clear in cold storage?

Anti-fog film is designed for chilled and fresh products where condensation would cloud a standard window.
