Kraft Paper vs White Cardstock Boxes

Your box color sets the tone before a customer reads a word.

Direct answer

Kraft boxes give a natural, earthy, eco feel with warm tones and slightly muted print; white cardstock gives a clean, bright, premium canvas with vivid color reproduction. Kraft is usually slightly cheaper; white cardstock prints more vibrantly and suits beauty, food and luxury brands.

RFQ checklist

  • Print colors and expected vibrancy

FAQ

Does printing look good on kraft boxes?

Print on kraft has a muted, organic look that suits natural brands. For bright brand colors, white cardstock or a coated inner liner is better.

Are kraft boxes cheaper?

Generally yes, kraft board costs less than coated white cardstock, and the savings grow at higher quantities.

Can kraft boxes be made food-grade?

Yes. Food-grade kraft with appropriate coatings is available; confirm requirements in the RFQ.

Can I get white inside and kraft outside?

Yes. Lined structures combine a kraft exterior with a white or printed interior.
