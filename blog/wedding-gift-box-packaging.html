Wedding Favor & Gift Box Packaging

Small boxes with a big personal touch.

Direct answer

Wedding favor boxes combine a compact structure, elegant finishes and personalization. Small folding cartons, mini rigid boxes or kraft boxes with printed stickers and ribbon suit favors, while larger gift boxes carry keepsakes and gifts. Personalize with names, dates and monograms.

FAQ

Can I print names and dates on favor boxes?

Yes, personalization with names, dates and monograms is standard on custom favor boxes.

What is the minimum quantity for wedding boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, which suits most weddings and events with extras.

What box suits small favors like chocolates?

Mini folding cartons or kraft favor boxes with a sticker seal are classic choices.

How early should I order?

Order at least a few weeks before the event to allow sampling, production and shipping.
