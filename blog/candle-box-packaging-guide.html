Candle Guide

Candle Packaging Boxes: Fire-Safe Cartons Guide

Heavy glass jars need boxes that protect and present.

Direct answer

Candle boxes must support heavy glass jars, hold the candle snugly during shipping, and carry clear labeling. Folding cartons with a corrugated insert or tray are standard; rigid boxes create a premium gifting feel. Confirm weight capacity, insert design and transit testing for large candles.

RFQ checklist

  • Candle weight and jar size
  • Box board strength and GSM
  • Insert or tray design
  • Labeling and warning print
  • Transit test requirement

FAQ

What box holds a heavy candle jar?

Use a folding carton with a corrugated tray or insert to distribute weight and prevent movement in transit.

Do candle boxes need special warning labels?

Candle products often require safety and burn warnings per destination-market rules; confirm labeling with your compliance team.

Can candle boxes be printed with full brand colors?

Yes, full-color print with matte or spot UV finishes is standard for candle brands.

What MOQ applies to candle boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies at Packaging Factory Direct, with samples available first.
