Pharma Carton Packaging: Compliance Basics

Pharmaceutical cartons carry compliance, not just branding.

Direct answer

Pharma cartons must support serialization (for example GS1 DataMatrix), tamper-evident features, regulatory text space and validated print quality. Confirm destination-market requirements, ink and material compliance, and any child-resistant or tamper-evident needs before production.

FAQ

What is serialization on pharma cartons?

Serialization adds unique codes (often GS1 DataMatrix) to each carton for track-and-trace compliance in regulated markets.

Do pharma cartons need special inks or materials?

Requirements vary by market; some require declared materials and compliant inks. Confirm before production.

Can you print small regulatory text clearly?

Yes, with validated print quality and appropriate plate and press settings for small text.

What MOQ applies to pharma cartons?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples and documentation available.
