Tea Box Packaging: Materials and Finishes

Tea sells with aroma, color and a premium feel.

Direct answer

Tea boxes need good moisture and aroma protection plus elegant print. Folding cartons with a foil-lined inner pouch or barrier liner are standard; window boxes display loose leaf; rigid and magnetic boxes carry premium gift lines. Confirm food-contact materials and barrier levels in the RFQ.

FAQ

How do I keep tea fresh in a box?

Use a foil-lined inner pouch or barrier liner inside the carton to protect aroma and moisture.

Are window boxes good for tea?

Yes, food-safe window film lets customers see the leaf, which builds trust and shelf appeal.

Can tea boxes be made food-grade?

Yes. Food-contact cartons and liners are available with compliance documents.

What MOQ applies to tea boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples available before mass production.
