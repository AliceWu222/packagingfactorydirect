FAQ

What makes packaging retail-ready?

Correct barcodes, compliant labels, strong front-panel design and practical shelf or display format.

Do retailers have packaging rules?

Yes, retailers often require specific barcode, label and packaging standards. Confirm with each retailer.

What is a display box?

A box designed to sit on a counter or shelf and present products for impulse purchase.

Can you help with barcode placement?

Yes, we can advise on barcode sizing and placement within your packaging design.
