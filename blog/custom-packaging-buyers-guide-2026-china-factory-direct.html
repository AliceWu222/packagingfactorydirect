Custom Packaging Buyer's Guide 2026: Sourcing Boxes, Bags & Pouches from a China Factory

By Packaging Factory Direct · Updated August 2026 · 12 min read

1. Why Buy Direct From a Factory (Not a Trading Company)

Packaging Factory Direct is a factory-direct manufacturer based in Longhua District, Shenzhen, China. We own the production lines for rigid boxes, folding cartons, magnetic boxes, stand-up pouches, paper bags, labels and printed paper products. Buying factory direct means the price you negotiate is the factory price — no trading-company margin — and the team answering your RFQ is the same team running the machines. MOQ starts from 500 PCS for most custom products, which fits both first-time importers and established brands.

2. The 6 Most-Sourced Packaging Categories in 2026

Across our export orders to the US, EU, Australia, the Middle East and Southeast Asia, six categories dominate B2B RFQs:

  • Custom rigid & magnetic boxes — luxury gift boxes, foldable magnetic boxes with ribbon and EVA inserts for cosmetics, jewellery and premium retail.
  • Stand-up & flat-bottom pouches — coffee bags with degassing valve, pet food bags, spout pouches, compostable and high-barrier structures.
  • Folding cartons & paper boxes — food boxes, pharma cartons, cosmetic cartons with foil stamping, soft-touch lamination and embossing.
  • Paper bags & retail packaging — kraft shopping bags, gift bags, thank-you cards and tissue paper for boutiques.
  • Labels, stickers & security labels — adhesive labels, holographic and anti-counterfeit labels, variable-data one-code-per-item serialization for pharma and medical aesthetics.
  • Containers & specialty packaging — PET food/pharma bottles, tin boxes, custom tape, mailer boxes, courier bags, zipper garment bags, bubble mailers.

3. Materials: What to Specify in Your RFQ

Tell your supplier the product weight, shelf life, barrier needs and the finish you want. Key options:

  • Paperboard & cardboard — white card (C1S/C2S), greyboard (for rigid boxes), kraft, corrugated (mailer boxes, shipping cartons). FSC® certified paper is available under our license FSC® C144065.
  • Flexible films — PET/AL/PE high-barrier lamination, single-material recyclable PE structures, matte or gloss finish, matte digital print or rotogravure.
  • Food-contact grades — FDA-compliant and EU 1935/2004 food-contact materials, with migration test reports such as XNQ Test Report XNO250418226BX2-1 available on request.

4. Printing, Finishes and Structural Options

Most of our custom packaging is printed flexo or rotogravure on flexible film, and offset or digital on paperboard. Popular finishes: foil stamping (gold, silver, holographic), spot UV, soft-touch matte lamination, embossing/debossing, silk-screen printing and laser-cut details. Structural options include magnetic closures, ribbon pulls, EVA/foam inserts, tuck-end or reverse-tuck cartons, and window patches.

5. Sampling, MOQ and Lead Times

Sampling is the fastest way to validate structure, colour and print quality. Typical lead times for our Shenzhen factory:

  • Dieline / structural drawing: free, within 24 hours of RFQ.
  • Physical sample: 3–7 days after artwork approval.
  • Mass production: 10–18 days depending on quantity, structure and finish.
  • MOQ: 500 PCS for most custom products; smaller trial orders can be discussed for new partnerships.

6. Compliance Documents You Can Request

Depending on your market, request these with your quotation: FDA food-contact test report, EU 1935/2004 declaration, FSC chain-of-custody confirmation, REACH substance declaration, material data sheets, AQL inspection report and pre-shipment photos. For pharma and medical-aesthetic packaging we support GS1 DataMatrix serialization, batch/lot coding and tamper-evident seals for one-code-per-item traceability.

7. RFQ Checklist: What to Send Us

A complete RFQ gets you a faster, more accurate quotation:

  • Product type and target market (food, cosmetics, pharma, retail…)
  • Dimensions (L×W×H) or fill volume/weight
  • Quantity and MOQ expectations
  • Material preference and print finish
  • Artwork files (PDF/AI) or your logo
  • Compliance needs (FDA, FSC, REACH, serialization…)
