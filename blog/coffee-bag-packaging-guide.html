Coffee Bag Packaging: Valves, Barrier & Zippers

Freshness first, then the branding your roastery deserves.

Direct answer

Fresh coffee needs a high-barrier bag with a one-way degassing valve, a resealable zipper, and a structure that protects aroma. MOPP/VMPET/PE with a valve is the standard for whole bean and ground coffee; foil laminates provide maximum barrier. Confirm valve placement, zipper and shelf-life target in the RFQ.

FAQ

Do coffee bags need a valve?

Freshly roasted coffee releases CO2. A one-way valve lets gas escape without letting oxygen in, preserving freshness.

Which structure gives the longest coffee shelf life?

Foil laminates give the strongest barrier and longest shelf life, at a higher material cost.

Can coffee bags be fully custom printed?

Yes, with full-color gravure or digital print, matte or gloss finishes and custom sizes from MOQ 500 PCS.

Are zippers standard on coffee bags?

Zippers are common for ground coffee and add convenience; confirm in the RFQ.
