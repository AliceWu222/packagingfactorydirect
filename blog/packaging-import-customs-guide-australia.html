Australia Packaging Import Guide

Australian importers face strict biosecurity and packaging rules.

Direct answer

Importing packaging to Australia needs customs documents, correct HS codes, biosecurity-compliant wood or paper packaging and awareness of APCO packaging regulations. Confirm wood-pallet treatment and packaging material declarations with your forwarder.

FAQ

What is ISPM-15?

A standard for treating wood packaging material (pallets, crates) to prevent pest spread. Non-compliant wood can be destroyed at the border.

Do Australian importers have packaging obligations?

Businesses may have obligations under APCO packaging initiatives; confirm applicability with a local advisor.

Are duties high on packaging imports?

Duty rates depend on HS classification; confirm with your broker and factor in GST.
