Gold Foil Stamping Cost Guide

What drives the price of foil stamping on packaging?

Direct answer

Foil stamping cost depends on foil area coverage, number of foil colors, whether a custom die is needed, and order quantity. A small logo stamp with a one-time die is affordable; large or full-coverage foil patterns multiply foil material and press time.

RFQ checklist

  • Foil color (gold, silver, rose gold, holographic)
  • Foil area and artwork vector file
  • Substrate and finish before foil
  • Expected quantity and reorder plan
  • Sample need for adhesion and color approval

FAQ

Is gold foil stamping expensive?

For a logo or small element, foil stamping adds a modest cost per unit plus a one-time die. It is one of the most cost-effective premium finishes.

Do I need a die for foil stamping?

Yes, a custom brass or copper die is made from your vector artwork. The cost is one-time and reused for reorders.

Can foil be combined with embossing?

Yes. Foil plus emboss creates a raised metallic effect and is a popular premium combination on rigid and folding boxes.

Does foil stamping work on kraft paper?

Yes, but adhesion and brightness differ on kraft. A sample test is recommended before mass production.
