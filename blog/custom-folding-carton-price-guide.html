Custom Folding Carton Price Guide

How is a custom folding carton priced from a packaging factory?

Direct answer

A folding carton price is built from paperboard grade and GSM, print coverage and process, finishes, die-cut and glue/stitch style, plus MOQ. Folding cartons are machine-produced and collapsible, which keeps unit cost low compared with rigid boxes at the same quantity.

Comparison table

Board grade (C1S, C2S, kraft)Coated boards cost more than uncoated kraftMatch board to shelf look and product weight

RFQ checklist

  • Product size and carton style (tuck-end, sleeve, tray)
  • Paperboard grade and GSM
  • Print colors and finish list
  • Window patch, inserts or special features
  • Quantity and pack-out requirement

FAQ

What is the cheapest folding carton style?

A straight-tuck or tuck-end carton with a single print pass is the most economical. Complex shapes and extra finishes increase cost.

Does printing affect folding carton price a lot?

Yes. Full-coverage 4-color print with lamination costs more than simple spot-color print. Reducing print coverage is one of the fastest ways to cut unit price.

Can folding cartons be made from recycled or kraft board?

Yes. Kraft and recycled boards are available and are often lower cost, though they change the visual result and print vibrancy.

What MOQ applies to custom folding cartons?

Packaging Factory Direct starts folding carton orders from MOQ 500 PCS, suitable for small launches and retail tests.
