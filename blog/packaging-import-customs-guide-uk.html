UK Packaging Import & EPR Basics

UK importers carry packaging responsibility obligations.

Direct answer

Importing packaging to the UK requires customs documents, correct HS codes, and, for most businesses, registration under the UK packaging EPR scheme with data reporting and fees. Confirm your obligation thresholds and registration with a UK compliance advisor.

FAQ

What is UK packaging EPR?

Extended Producer Responsibility for packaging requires businesses placing packaging on the UK market to register, report data and pay fees.

Do I need to register if I import small volumes?

Registration depends on thresholds. Check the current rules with a UK compliance advisor.

What are pEPR fees?

Per-unit fees charged on packaging materials placed on the market, based on material type and recyclability.

Can you help with UK import documents?

We provide accurate packing data and HS code support for your broker and compliance process.
