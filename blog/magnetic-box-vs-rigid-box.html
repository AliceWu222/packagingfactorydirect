Magnetic Box vs Rigid Box: What's the Difference?

Both feel premium, but they open and close very differently.

Direct answer

A magnetic box is a rigid box with a magnetic closure, usually a lid that swings open. A standard rigid box has a removable or hinge-lid without magnets. Magnetic boxes add closure hardware and assembly, so they cost slightly more but create a more tactile, premium open-close experience.

FAQ

Are magnetic boxes worth the extra cost?

For brands where the open-close moment matters, the magnetic snap strongly improves perceived quality and can support a higher retail price.

Can magnets be a problem in shipping?

Magnets are safe for shipping, but declare them correctly in some air freight contexts. Confirm with your forwarder for large volumes.

Do magnetic boxes ship flat?

No. Like rigid boxes, they are pre-assembled, so freight volume is higher than collapsible cartons.
