Audit Guide

How to Audit a Packaging Factory: Checklist

Verify the factory before you commit a production order.

Direct answer

Audit the factory on four levels: documents, production, quality control and communication. Check certifications, on-site production lines, QC procedures, sample capability and response quality. A real factory shows you evidence, not just promises.

Comparison table

Audit areaWhat to checkRed flag
DocumentsBusiness license, certificationsRefusal to share
ProductionMachines, lines, capacityOnly photos, no video
Quality controlQC steps, inline checksNo QC process described
SamplesSample speed and qualityDelayed or poor samples
CommunicationResponse time, detailVague or pushy sales

RFQ checklist

  • Request business and certification documents
  • Ask for live production evidence
  • Review QC process and reports
  • Order samples and test them
  • Check communication quality

FAQ

How do I verify a packaging factory is real?

Check documents, ask for live video of production, order samples, and verify the factory through trade platforms and references.

Which certifications matter for packaging?

ISO, food-contact compliance, FSC for paper sourcing, and destination-market certifications as applicable.

Should I audit before or after samples?

Order samples first, then audit before mass production. A good sample from a verified factory is the safest path.

Can I visit your factory?

Yes, Packaging Factory Direct welcomes buyer visits and provides factory capability evidence on request.
