Import Guide

Canada Packaging Import Guide

Plan bilingual labeling and duty from day one.

Direct answer

Importing packaging to Canada requires standard customs documents, correct HS codes and, for retail products, English and French bilingual labeling. Food-contact packaging must meet Canadian food safety requirements. Confirm labeling and compliance with your advisor before printing.

FAQ

Do Canadian labels need to be bilingual?

Retail products sold in Canada generally require English and French labeling. Confirm with a compliance advisor.

What duties apply to packaging imports?

Duties depend on HS classification and trade agreements; confirm with your broker.

Are food-contact rules different in Canada?

Canada has its own food packaging safety requirements; request compliance documents from your supplier.

Can you help with Canada import documents?

We provide accurate packing data and HS code support for your broker.
