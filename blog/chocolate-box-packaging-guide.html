Chocolate Box Packaging: Food-Safe Materials

Keep chocolate beautiful from factory to unboxing.

Direct answer

Chocolate boxes need food-safe board, an inner liner or tray that prevents chocolate-to-board contact where required, and moisture protection because chocolate is sensitive to humidity and temperature. Folding cartons with a PET tray or grease-resistant liner are standard; rigid boxes carry premium and gift formats.

RFQ checklist

  • Food contact requirement and standards
  • Chocolate type and temperature sensitivity
  • Insert or tray design
  • Moisture barrier need
  • MOQ and lead time

FAQ

Do chocolate boxes need food-grade board?

Board that contacts chocolate should be food-safe; a PET tray or food-grade liner is the common solution.

How do I prevent chocolate melting in transit?

Insulated shipping and season planning matter more than the box itself. The box should be moisture-resistant to protect quality.

Can chocolate boxes have windows?

Yes, with food-grade window film, which is popular for retail chocolate display.

What is the MOQ for chocolate boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples and food-contact documents available.
