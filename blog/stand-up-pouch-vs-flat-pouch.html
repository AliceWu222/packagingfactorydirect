Stand Up Pouch vs Flat Pouch: Which to Choose?

Two flexible packaging formats with different shelf presence.

Direct answer

Stand up pouches stand on shelves with a gusseted bottom and give more display area; flat pouches lie flat, cost less and ship more compactly. Choose stand up for retail shelf products and brand display, flat for cost-sensitive, boxed or mail-order products.

RFQ checklist

  • Retail display or mail-order use
  • Fill weight and product type
  • Barrier requirement
  • Zipper, spout or other features
  • Quantity and shelf life target

FAQ

Why do stand up pouches cost more?

They use more film area, a gusseted bottom and more converting steps, which raises material and labor cost per unit.

Can a flat pouch still look premium?

Yes. With strong print design and matte or soft-touch finish, flat pouches work well for refill and premium mail-order formats.

Do stand up pouches need a bottom gusset?

Yes, the gusset is what lets the pouch stand. It also adds a small amount of extra capacity.

Can I use either format for food?

Yes, both can be made food-grade. Confirm the barrier and food-contact requirements in the RFQ.
