Payment Guide

Payment Terms with Packaging Factories

Standard terms protect both buyer and factory.

Direct answer

Most factories use T/T with a deposit (commonly 30%) and the balance before shipment, or platform trade assurance for protection. Negotiate terms by order size and relationship, and confirm them in the contract before production.

TermHow it worksProtection
T/T 30/7030% deposit, 70% before shipmentStandard, flexible

RFQ checklist

  • Confirm payment terms in writing
  • Use trade assurance when available
  • Keep deposits proportionate to order
  • Confirm balance timing before shipment
  • Document everything

FAQ

Is a 30% deposit standard?

Yes, 30% deposit with the balance before shipment is common for custom packaging orders.

Can I pay by credit card or PayPal?

Platform payment options vary; confirm acceptable methods with the factory.

What protects me if the order is wrong?

Trade assurance, signed contracts and pre-shipment inspection reports protect buyers.

What payment terms do you offer?

Packaging Factory Direct offers T/T and platform-protected payments depending on the order.
