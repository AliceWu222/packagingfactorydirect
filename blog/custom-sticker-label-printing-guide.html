Sticker Guide

Custom Stickers & Labels for Packaging

Labels carry compliance, stickers carry personality.

Direct answer

Custom stickers and labels add branding, compliance and protection to packaging. Choose material by use: paper labels for most boxes, vinyl or synthetic for moisture resistance, and clear film for no-label look. Confirm die-cut shape, finish and adhesion surface in the RFQ.

RFQ checklist

  • Print colors
  • Quantity

FAQ

What sticker is best for box seals?

Paper stickers are economical and recyclable; holographic stickers add a premium, anti-copy look.

Can labels be die-cut to custom shapes?

Yes, custom die-cut shapes are standard for stickers and labels.

Do I need waterproof labels?

For bottles, food or outdoor products, vinyl or synthetic labels resist moisture and scuffing.

What MOQ applies to custom stickers?

Sticker orders are flexible and can accompany your packaging order from MOQ 500 PCS.
