Holiday Packaging: Seasonal Design & Timing

Seasonal packaging wins sales only if it arrives on time.

Direct answer

Holiday packaging needs planning backwards from the season. Festive colors, seasonal patterns and limited-edition messaging lift sales, but production and shipping lead times mean ordering months ahead. Reuse the base structure with seasonal print to control cost.

FAQ

How early should I order holiday packaging?

Order 2-4 months ahead of the season to cover design, sampling, production and ocean or air freight.

How do I keep seasonal packaging affordable?

Reuse the base structure and change only the print, avoiding new tooling each season.

Can I do a limited-edition holiday design?

Yes, limited-edition prints on the same structure keep cost low and create urgency.

What MOQ applies to seasonal packaging?

MOQ 500 PCS applies per design at Packaging Factory Direct.
