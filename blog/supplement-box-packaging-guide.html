Supplement Bottle & Box Packaging Guide

Compliance and shelf appeal for health brands.

Direct answer

Supplement cartons protect bottles, carry required compliance labeling and build trust on shelves. Folding cartons with a snug tray or insert are standard; verify labeling space, ink requirements and destination-market rules before printing.

FAQ

Do supplement boxes need special printing?

Labeling requirements vary by market (for example FDA-style supplement facts panels in the US). Confirm layout and ink needs early.

What material is best for supplement cartons?

Coated folding carton board (300-350 GSM) with matte lamination balances print quality, strength and cost.

Can supplements be packaged in pouches?

Yes, for powders and gummies, with food-contact films and proper barrier for shelf life.

What is the MOQ for supplement cartons?

MOQ 500 PCS applies, with samples and compliance documents available.
