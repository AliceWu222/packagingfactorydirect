Window Box vs Open Box: Product Display Packaging

Let customers see the product, or keep it protected?

Direct answer

Window boxes use a clear film patch so customers see the product while it stays protected and clean; open boxes leave the product exposed for tactile display. Windows add cost and a production step; open boxes are cheaper but suit only products that do not need protection.

FAQ

How much more does a window cost?

A window adds film material and an application step, increasing unit cost modestly compared with a plain box.

Are windows food-safe?

Yes, food-grade PET or cellophane window film is available. Confirm food-contact compliance in the RFQ.

Can the window be a custom shape?

Yes, windows can be die-cut in circles, ovals, cutouts or full panels.

Do window boxes protect the product?

The film patch seals the opening, protecting the product from dust and handling while keeping it visible.
