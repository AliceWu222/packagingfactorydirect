Jewelry Guide

Jewelry Packaging Boxes for Premium Brands

Boxes that make jewelry feel even more precious.

Direct answer

Jewelry packaging uses rigid boxes with velvet, leatherette or flocked interiors, foam inserts and often magnetic closures. The box must protect delicate pieces, prevent tarnishing contact and create a keepsake unboxing experience. Folding cartons with flocked inserts suit budget lines; rigid boxes carry hero collections.

FAQ

What interior is best for jewelry boxes?

Velvet or flocked interiors protect delicate pieces and prevent scratching; leatherette adds a premium finish.

Do jewelry boxes need anti-tarnish features?

For silver and reactive metals, anti-tarnish liners or tabs can be added to slow tarnishing.

Can I get custom foam slots for rings and earrings?

Yes, foam inserts with custom cavities hold each piece securely.

What MOQ applies to jewelry boxes?

MOQ 500 PCS applies at Packaging Factory Direct, with samples available first.
