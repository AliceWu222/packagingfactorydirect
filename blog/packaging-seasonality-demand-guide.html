Packaging Demand Cycles & Planning

Plan packaging around your sales calendar.

Direct answer

Packaging demand follows retail and ecommerce cycles: holiday peaks, product launches and seasonal categories. Plan orders 2-4 months ahead of demand peaks, lock structures early and order at price tiers to balance cost and availability.

FAQ

When should I order packaging for the holidays?

Order 3-4 months before the peak selling season to cover production and shipping.

How do I plan for a product launch?

Secure packaging stock before the launch date, including buffer for delays.

Does ordering early save money?

Yes, planning ahead lets you order at better price tiers and avoid rush fees.

Can you advise on ordering timing?

Yes, we can share lead time guidance to support your demand planning.
