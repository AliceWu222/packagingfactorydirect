Case Studies

Custom Packaging Case Studies & Project Examples

How brands across cosmetics, food, coffee, ecommerce and retail launch custom packaging with MOQ 500 PCS: from dieline and sampling to production, QC and worldwide delivery.

Cosmetics Brand: Skincare Box With Foam Insert

A skincare brand launching a serum line needed a carton with a snug foam insert and matte lamination with spot UV. The project started with a dieline review, moved to a physical sample for color approval, then produced 1,000 cartons with pre-shipment inspection and air freight delivery.

Food Brand: Custom Coffee Bags With Valve

A specialty coffee roaster ordered stand up pouches with a one-way degassing valve and zipper. Film structure, valve placement and print proof were confirmed on sample, then production followed with food-contact documents for the destination market.

Ecommerce Brand: Printed Mailer Boxes

An ecommerce brand consolidated multiple SKUs into a family of printed mailer boxes with inside print. Right-sizing reduced void fill and freight, and the base structure was reused for seasonal print changes without new tooling.

Retail Brand: Gift Boxes & Paper Bags

A retail brand paired rigid magnetic gift boxes with matching paper bags for a seasonal campaign. Both were produced from MOQ 500 PCS with foil logo stamping, and delivery was timed around the campaign launch.

Pharma-Adjacent: Serialized Cartons

For a regulated product launch, cartons were produced with GS1 DataMatrix serialization and tamper-evident seals, with print validation and documentation provided before shipment.

What Every Project Shares

  1. Clear specification: structure, material, finish, quantity and timeline.
  2. Dieline and artwork review before sampling.
  3. Physical sample approval covering size, color and insert fit.
  4. Locked specs, in-line QC and pre-shipment inspection.
  5. Worldwide shipping by sea, air or express.
