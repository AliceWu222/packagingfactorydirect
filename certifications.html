Certifications & Compliance

Packaging Certifications & Compliance Support

Documented compliance matters for food, cosmetics, pharma and retail packaging. Packaging Factory Direct supports food-grade materials, FSC-certified paper options, REACH declarations and ISO-aligned quality management, with documents provided on request.

Food-Grade Compliance

Food-contact packaging can be produced with food-grade papers and coatings compliant with FDA and EU 1935/2004 frameworks. Migration test reports and material declarations are available on request for applicable products.

FSC-Certified Paper Options

FSC-certified paper and board can be specified for boxes, cartons and bags. Confirm availability and certificate documentation when requesting a quote for sustainability-sensitive markets.

REACH & Substance Declarations

Material declarations supporting REACH compliance are available for EU-bound packaging, including ink and coating substance information where applicable.

ISO-Aligned Quality Management

Production follows an ISO-aligned quality management approach: incoming material checks, in-line inspection, AQL sampling and pre-shipment inspection with reports provided to buyers.

Pharma & Serialization Support

Pharma cartons can include GS1 DataMatrix serialization, batch/lot coding and tamper-evident seals, with print validation and documentation on request.

How to Request Compliance Documents

  1. Mention the destination market and product type in your RFQ.
  2. List the documents you need: material declarations, migration tests, FSC certificates, REACH declarations.
  3. We confirm availability, arrange samples and provide the documents before mass production.
